# 秒燃（Seconds Burn）隐私政策

**生效日期：2026 年 9 月 26 日**

我们非常重视您的隐私。本应用"秒燃"（以下简称"本应用"）是一款间歇训练计时工具。本政策说明本应用如何处理（以及如何不处理）您的信息。

## 一、我们不收集您的个人信息

- 本应用**无需注册或登录**，不收集您的姓名、邮箱、电话、位置或任何身份识别信息。
- 本应用**不嵌入任何第三方统计、广告或追踪 SDK**，不向任何第三方共享或出售数据。
- 本应用**不进行用户行为分析或画像**。

## 二、数据的本地存储

- 您的训练计划、训练记录、成就勋章、昵称与头像等数据**仅存储在您的设备本地**（应用沙盒内）。
- 头像图片经压缩后保存在设备本地，不会上传到任何服务器。

## 三、设备内同步

- 本应用通过系统提供的 WatchConnectivity 功能，在**您本人的 iPhone 与 Apple Watch 之间**同步训练计划与记录，该过程不经过任何外部服务器。
- iPhone 与 Apple Watch 之间通过系统"应用组"共享必要的应用数据，仅限您本人的设备。

## 四、通知

- 如果您开启"每日训练提醒"，本应用将在您设备本地发送一条定时提醒通知。您可随时在应用内或系统设置中关闭。

## 五、系统备份

- 如果您在系统层面开启了 iCloud 备份，本地数据可能随系统备份由 Apple 管理，该过程受 Apple 隐私政策约束，与本应用无关。

## 六、儿童隐私

本应用不面向 14 岁以下儿童进行营销或收集数据，也不会有意收集儿童的任何个人信息。

## 七、您的权利

- 由于数据仅存储在本地，您删除本应用即会**彻底删除**全部相关数据。
- 您也可以随时在应用内修改或清除您的昵称与头像。

## 八、政策变更

若本政策发生重大变更，我们将在应用更新中同步更新本页面并标注生效日期。

## 九、联系我们

如对本政策有任何疑问，请联系：**2482881998@qq.com**

# Seconds Burn Privacy Policy

**Effective Date: September 26, 2026**

Your privacy matters to us. "Seconds Burn" (the "App") is an interval training timer. This policy explains how the App handles — and does not handle — your information.

## 1. We Do Not Collect Personal Information

- The App **requires no sign-up or login** and does not collect your name, email, phone number, location, or any identifying information.
- The App **contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs**, and never shares or sells data to third parties.
- The App **does not profile users or analyze behavior**.

## 2. Local Data Storage

- Your workout plans, records, medals, nickname and avatar are stored **only on your device** (within the app sandbox).
- Avatar images are compressed and saved locally; they are never uploaded to any server.

## 3. On-Device Sync

- The App uses the system-provided WatchConnectivity framework to sync plans and records **between your own iPhone and Apple Watch**. This process does not involve any external server.
- Necessary app data is shared between iPhone and Apple Watch via the system App Group, limited to your own devices.

## 4. Notifications

- If you enable the "Daily Reminder", the App schedules a local notification on your device. You can disable it anytime in the App or system settings.

## 5. System Backups

- If you enable iCloud Backup at the system level, local data may be included in backups managed by Apple, governed by Apple's Privacy Policy, not by this App.

## 6. Children's Privacy

The App is not marketed to children under 14 and does not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

## 7. Your Rights

- Because data is stored only on your device, **deleting the App permanently erases** all related data.
- You can modify or remove your nickname and avatar anytime within the App.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes materially, we will update this page with the app update and note the effective date.
